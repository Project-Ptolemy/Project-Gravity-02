A SMALL GUIDE TO BIG IDEAS

Meet your shape studio.

1

Draw and reshape

Draw a path or sketch freehand. Edit points in XY, XZ or YZ. Use Select or Shift-click for groups of vertices, then move, rotate, scale or flatten the selection. Choose Apply path to keep your edits.

2

Build on your ideas

Import SVG or coordinates, generate geometry with formulas, or start with a polygon. Make a surface extrudes or revolves a path. Repeat / arrange creates editable copies in a line or around an axis.

3

Bring it to life

Add spin, orbit, pulse or waves. Use Controls for sliders, switches and action buttons. Save an editable JSON project, then export your Lua plugin for the game.
